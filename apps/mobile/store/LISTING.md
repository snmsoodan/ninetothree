### Description

Nine to Three is one calm place for the whole of early parenthood: pregnancy week by week, then your child's first three years.

Each week gives you three things to do, the food and sleep that matter just now, the appointments that are coming, and the questions worth asking at them. When something worries you, the triage screen asks three questions and then tells you whether to call your provider tonight or watch and wait until morning.

Everything here is written for a tired person at 3am. Short sentences. No cheerleading. No promises nobody can keep. Where a claim comes from a guideline or a study, the source is on the page.

WHAT IS INSIDE

- A page for this week, with the few things that actually matter now
- Every stage from pregnancy week 4 to the third birthday
- Nutrition, sleep, feeding, clothes, gear, childcare, and your own recovery
- Trips to the provider, with the questions to ask and the signs that mean go now
- A library of 251 hand-checked links, so an answer is one tap away
- A shelf for the things you pinned, plus appointments and notes
- Together: a partner sees the same week on their own phone

WHAT IT COSTS

Nothing. No subscription, no ads, no upsell, no account required to read a week. Sign in only if you want your shelf on more than one device.

WHAT IT IS NOT

It is not a substitute for your provider. It never diagnoses, never doses a medicine, and when the answer is "call", it says call.

Nine to Three is made by a small independent team. If a page is wrong, there is a corrections form in the app and a person reads it.

### What is new

The first release: pregnancy to age three, the week page, triage, the library, the shelf, and Together.
